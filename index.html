About

Who's building this

I'm an electrical engineering student at the University of Alberta who mostly learns by building the thing and finding out where it breaks.

The projects here weren't assignments. The lamp exists because flickering PWM annoyed me. The environmental monitor exists because I wanted to know if I could make something run for a month untouched. Momentum exists because every focus app on the market was designed for a brain that isn't mine.

What I actually enjoy is where the schematic meets reality — where the datasheet said one thing and the oscilloscope says another, and you have to work out which one is lying. I'd rather understand why a circuit works than memorise that it does.

Looking for a summer co-op where I can do more of that, with people who are better at it than me.
