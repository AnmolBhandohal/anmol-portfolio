    About

    Two kinds of electrical work

    I split my time between two versions of electrical work. At school it's op-amp filters, VHDL and PCB stackups. On site it's conduit, feeders, and a fire alarm panel that has to pass inspection.

    Most students only get the first. The second taught me things a lab never does: a connector that's easy to crimp can be impossible to reach in the finished install; a fault can hide until the circuit is live; and a drawing is a promise the building then has to keep. That's why my boards use connectors that can't go in backwards.

    One rule for this site: nothing on it I haven't actually done. The lamp isn't finished, so it shows design targets, labelled as targets. Measured results go up when they've been measured.
